The Event

Opportunity begins with a choice.

The Exclusive Grand Opening is The Zetsumetsu Corporation's limited sponsorship initiative. Selected individuals gain access to a development pathway built around what they want to achieve.

01 / SUPPORT

Financial Assistance

Up to $600 in sponsorship funding for approved educational or business ventures, progressively available through completed milestones.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

A Path to Follow

Participants plan, develop, publish, and learn through practical work. Zetsu EDU provides educational assessment and certificates for completed milestones.

03 / INDEPENDENCE

Your Work Remains Yours

Participants retain ownership of their creations. Each stage is a choice. Continuing to the next is never required.
